## XML namespace and referenced standards

The six-schema scope includes the
[pinned xml.xsd dependency](https://github.com/w3c-cg/musicxml/blob/799e2defb2ece0ae7bafe08dcbcac25b2c631d53/schema/xml.xsd).
It identifies its XML namespace sources and the dated
[W3C XML namespace schema](https://www.w3.org/2007/08/xml.xsd).
That dependency's provenance is distinct from the MusicXML-owned modules; this
repository does not assign it a new license.

The registry also references W3C XML, XML Namespaces, XML Schema, and other
external standards. Those references and any source-derived material retain the
rights and terms identified by their respective source publications. Follow the
per-record `source_url`, related evidence locators, and source provenance for the
exact document and version. External standards are not bundled wholesale or
relicensed here.

## Material not included

No complete upstream schemas, raw HTML cache, downloaded source archive,
MusicXML score corpus, MusicXML Test Suite, or third-party implementation code is
bundled in this standalone repository. The original go-musicxml test-fixture
notices therefore are not presented as licenses for files that are absent here.

When adding third-party material, preserve its complete applicable notices and
record its source, version, scope, and terms separately from project-owned work.
